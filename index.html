About Us

Speedy Insurance Agency

Speedy Insurance Agency has delivered insurance coverage to satisfied clients for over 20 years. We research and review rates from hundreds of companies to find our customers the best rates available — we want each client to have the cheapest rates with quality coverage.

Our services include auto, motorcycle, home, recreational vehicles, general liability, DMV services, SR-22 filings, bonds, and commercial insurance. Our team of licensed and dedicated professional agents are committed above all to customer trust.

We love helping new clients explore their options and match policies to their personal needs. Our top priority is confident, satisfied customers. Speedy Insurance Agency was founded — and is still fueled by — a vision to educate and assist clients navigating the complex world of insurance. Insurance shopping doesn't have to be difficult.

Our bilingual team speaks English and Spanish. We are open Mon–Fri 9am–7pm, Sat 10am–5pm, Sun 10am–5pm (Moreno Valley only), across 5 locations in Moreno Valley, Riverside, Lake Elsinore, and Colton.

Confidentiality & Security

We maintain strict physical, electronic, and procedural safeguards to protect your information from unauthorized access by third parties. We use cookies on our website to improve your experience; most browsers allow you to control cookie settings.

California Residents (CCPA)

California residents have the following rights:

  • Right to Know — Request what personal information we have collected about you over the past 12 months.
  • Right to Delete — Request that we delete the information we have collected from you.
  • Right to Opt-Out — We do not sell your information to any third parties.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination — You will not be discriminated against for exercising any of these rights.

To exercise your California privacy rights, email us at info@speedyins.com or call toll-free (866) 577-3339. We aim to respond within 45 days.
