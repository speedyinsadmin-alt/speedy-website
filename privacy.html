Who we are

Speedy Insurance Agency, Inc. ("Speedy," "we") is an independent insurance agency operating branch offices in Southern California. This policy covers our website (speedyins.com), our client payment pages, and our internal payment tools, including the Speedy Payment Bridge application used with Clover devices at our offices.

Information we collect

We collect information you provide to obtain insurance quotes and service your policies (such as name, contact details, and policy information) and information generated when you make a payment to us (amount, date, purpose, and payment confirmation details).

Payment card information

Card payments are processed by Clover / Fiserv, our payment processor. Card numbers are entered directly with Clover through secure fields or on Clover terminals. Speedy never sees or stores your full card number. We retain only confirmation details (card brand, last four digits, transaction and authorization identifiers) to document your payment.

How we use information

We use information to provide quotes, service policies, process and document payments, comply with insurance and financial record-keeping requirements, and communicate with you about your account. We do not sell personal information.

Sharing

Information is shared only as needed with insurance carriers to quote and service your policies, with our payment processor to process payments, and with service providers who support our operations under confidentiality obligations, or as required by law.

California privacy rights (CCPA)

California residents may request access to or deletion of their personal information by contacting us at info@speedyins.com or (951) 472-0927. We do not sell personal information and do not discriminate against consumers who exercise their privacy rights.

Security & retention

We use industry-standard safeguards, restrict internal tools to authorized employees, and retain records only as long as required for insurance and financial compliance.
