Commercial Auto Liability

Injury and damage while transporting passengers. Brokers require $1,000,000 combined single limit for vans up to 7 passengers — higher for larger vehicles.

Abuse & Molestation

The endorsement every Medi-Cal broker now requires, typically $1,000,000. Most standard commercial auto policies leave it out; ours includes it.

Lift & Equipment

General Liability

California Requirements

What Medi-Cal And
The Brokers Ask For

DHCS Medi-Cal provider enrollmentEnrollment through the PAVE portal: commercial vehicle registration, proof of commercial insurance, driver licenses, first aid and CPR cards, drug and alcohol tests, and the MCSA-5875/5876 medical forms for each driver.
Vehicle Safety Systems Inspection (VSSI)Since March 27, 2025 every NEMT vehicle needs a VSSI certificate from a Bureau of Automotive Repair station before enrollment. We tell you what the inspector looks at.
Broker certificate requirementsThe managed-care plans route trips through transportation brokers, and each broker publishes its own certificate rules: limits, endorsements, the additional insured wording. We write to the broker you work with.
Commercial registration and driver filesEvery van registered commercially, every driver with a clean DMV printout on file. We pull the MVRs before we quote so there are no surprises at binding.
